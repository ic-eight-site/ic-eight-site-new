Product Value Evaluation Experiments

What does AI believe a product is — and how does that change when the evidence changes?

A PVE is not a product review. It evaluates what value a first-time user can actually discover from the product, using evidence rather than personal preference.

An ongoing research series evaluating real products through public positioning, first-use evidence, direct product observation, and capability evidence. Each evaluation also examines where AI's conclusions hold, where they fail, and where human judgment changes the decision.

Two ways PVE is used

Editorial PVE is independently selected and published by IC Eight.

Commissioned PVE is a paid private evaluation of how a product's value is perceived from the outside. It does not provide strategic direction or implementation recommendations. Publication is optional and requires separate agreement.

How this relates to IC Eight

IC Eight works with companies looking toward Japan, while Product Value Evaluation begins one layer earlier: with the product itself. Some evaluations focus on that core product layer; others extend into how its value is discovered, understood, and trusted in Japan.

About the experiment

This is an editorial research series, not an automated version of IC Eight's Product Value Diagnosis.

IC Eight's client diagnosis is conducted through direct product use, evidence review, and human judgment. These experiments examine what AI can discover, how its conclusions change with the evidence, and where judgment still matters.
