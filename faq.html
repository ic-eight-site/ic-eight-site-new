FAQ

Common questions.

Working together

Do we need a long-term contract?

No. Most teams start with a focused diagnostic around one product, market, or question. If deeper work is needed, we scope it separately based on what we find.

How do projects usually start?

Most projects start with a product, a market, and a question. Tell us what you believe the product should make users understand or value, and what you expect to happen in Japan. We use the real product, follow the evidence, and identify where that intended value diverges from what users can actually discover.

Do we need to have launched in Japan already?

No. We can examine the product before launch, during early market entry, or after launch when something is not landing as expected.

What do you actually examine?

It depends on the question and what the evidence points to. We may examine first use, positioning, trust signals, onboarding, product messaging, help content, localization, or the surfaces around the product that shape what users understand before they commit.

Do you implement the changes you recommend?

Not automatically. The initial engagement is diagnostic: we identify the gap, determine what matters most, and recommend a direction. If the diagnosis points to deeper work, that can be scoped separately.

We already have a Japanese product. Can you still help?

Yes. Existing Japanese content is often part of what we examine, but not necessarily the whole problem. The gap may be in positioning, first use, trust signals, product structure, or the content system itself.

Localization & content systems

Where does localization fit?

Teams often come to us because they suspect a localization or UX problem. We do not assume that is the root cause. We diagnose whether the real issue is localization, positioning, product experience, trust, or something else. Localization becomes part of the work when language or the content system is where product value gets lost. In those cases, we may examine Japanese UX, product messaging, onboarding, help content, or the broader localization system.

What was the AI-Native Localization Framework?

It was an early-2026 term IC Eight used for generating localized product content from intent, product context, and constraints rather than treating the English source as a sentence-by-sentence template. It has since been folded into IC Eight's broader Product Value Discovery practice. The original definitions are preserved for reference: AI-Native Localization Framework and Intent-Based Generation.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on scope. We confirm the timeline before the project starts.
