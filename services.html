How We Work

How we find the gap.

Not a checklist of fixes. A diagnosis of what your product is trying to communicate, what users actually discover, and what should change next.

How the diagnosis narrows

We don't start with solutions.

We narrow the problem first.

01

What are you trying to sell?

The claim, the audience, and the value users are meant to discover.

02

What should users discover?

What they need to understand for the product to work in this market.

03

What do they actually see?

We use the real product as a first-time user would, and follow the surrounding surfaces when the evidence points us there.

04

Where does that diverge?

We compare intended value with what's actually discoverable, and identify where the meaning drops, shifts, or disappears.

05

What matters enough to change?

We return the diagnosis, the priorities, and the direction for what should change next.

What happens next?

Depends on what we find.

Deeper work is scoped separately, based on the diagnosis — not before.
