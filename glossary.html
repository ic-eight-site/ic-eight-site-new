Glossary

Terms IC Eight uses — and what they mean.

These terms are sometimes used loosely in the industry. This is how IC Eight defines them.

Product Value Discovery is IC Eight's overall approach. Product Value Diagnosis is a scoped engagement and deliverable that applies that approach to a specific product. The five terms below build on that distinction, in order.

Product Value Discovery

The process of examining the value a company intends its product to offer, the value users can actually discover through the product experience, and where the two diverge. Product Value Discovery may also reveal stronger or different value that the team did not originally intend to emphasize.

Intended Value

The value a company believes its product offers and expects users to recognize. It may be expressed through positioning, feature emphasis, onboarding, or messaging — but it is broader than a written value proposition. Intended value can also be embedded directly in the structure and experience of the product itself.

Discovered Value

The value that actually becomes visible, understandable, credible, and usable to users through the product experience. It may differ from the value the company intended to emphasize. Discovered value refers specifically to value users can discover — not value discovered by AI, or by IC Eight.

First-Use Value

The value a first-time user can realistically discover during their initial experience with the product, before deciding whether there is enough reason to continue or return. The question is whether the first use proves enough value to earn another one — not the full value the product may eventually offer.

Product Value Diagnosis

A scoped, decision-oriented application of Product Value Discovery to a specific product. It identifies what value the product actually proves, where that diverges from what the team intended, what matters enough to change, and what can wait. A diagnosis returns priority and direction — not only observations.

Localization, Japanese UX, positioning, trust, first use, and content systems remain diagnostic surfaces IC Eight looks at — not separate categories of work. When a diagnosis finds that content generation is where value is being lost, IC Eight applies AI-native generation as one downstream response, not as the starting assumption.
